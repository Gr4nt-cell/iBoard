# Word lists

- `<lang>.txt`: word frequencies from hermitdave/FrequencyWords (2018, OpenSubtitles),
  https://github.com/hermitdave/FrequencyWords, content licensed CC BY-SA 4.0.
  Filtered and trimmed by tools/gen_assets.py.
- `zh_pinyin.tsv`: Chinese words and frequencies from fxsjy/jieba `dict.txt` (MIT),
  https://github.com/fxsjy/jieba, with toneless pinyin from mozillazg/python-pinyin (MIT),
  https://github.com/mozillazg/python-pinyin. Built by tools/build_pinyin.py.
