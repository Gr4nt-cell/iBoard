iBoard keyboard for Android

Privacy Policy

Effective 16 October 2026 · Applies to the iBoard app (package com.gr4nt.iboard)

In short

  • Everything you type is processed on your phone. iBoard has no servers, no accounts, no ads and no analytics.
  • Optional AI features send text to the AI provider you choose, using your own API key, only after you agree in the app.
  • AI features are never used in password fields or fields marked private by the app you are typing in.
  • Learned words, clipboard history and settings stay on your phone and are deleted when you uninstall iBoard.

Who we are

iBoard is developed by Gr4nt. Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to gstephen116@gmail.com.

What stays on your phone

These are stored only in iBoard's private app storage on your device. We never receive them.

  • Learned words. Words and word pairs you type, used to improve predictions. You can turn this off or clear it in Settings › Typing.
  • Clipboard history. Your last 40 copied items, so you can paste them again. Items an app marks as sensitive are skipped. Turn it off in Settings › Clipboard, or clear it from the clipboard panel.
  • Emoji history and skin-tone choices.
  • Settings, including your AI API key if you add one.
  • An emoji font you import, if you choose to. It is copied into iBoard's private storage and is not shared.

Optional AI features

AI word prediction, Translate and Fix Grammar are off by default. They only work after you add an API key from an AI provider and agree to the in-app disclosure. When they are on:

  • Word prediction sends up to the last few hundred characters before your cursor, plus the word you are typing.
  • Translate and Fix Grammar send the text you selected, or the whole text field if nothing is selected, when you tap the action.
  • Text goes directly from your phone to the provider you picked: Anthropic (Claude), or an OpenAI-compatible service at the address you enter. It does not pass through any iBoard server.
  • The provider processes the text under its own privacy policy and terms. Please review them; for example, Anthropic's Privacy Policy.
  • iBoard never sends text from password fields or from fields that request no personalized learning (such as incognito browser tabs).

You can turn AI features off at any time with the ✨ button or in Settings › AI Predictions, and withdraw your consent there.

Voice typing

When you tap the microphone, iBoard uses your phone's built-in speech recognition service (for example, Google's) to turn speech into text. Audio is handled by that service under its own policy. iBoard does not record or store audio.

Offline Dictation. If you download the optional offline packs (Settings › Offline Dictation), speech recognition and the clean-up of your dictation run entirely on your phone. Audio is processed in memory while you speak and is never saved or sent anywhere. The packs are open models (OpenAI Whisper, Qwen2.5) downloaded once from Hugging Face; downloading them shares only your IP address with Hugging Face, as any download does.

Sharing and selling

We do not sell, rent or share your data. We do not use advertising or tracking SDKs. The only data that leaves your phone is the text you choose to send to your AI provider, as described above.

Keeping or deleting your data

Because your data stays on your device, you control it. Use Settings › Typing › Reset Learned Words, clear the clipboard panel, or remove an imported font. Uninstalling iBoard deletes everything it stored. We hold no copy to delete.

Children

iBoard is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above. Significant changes to what leaves your phone will also be shown in the app before they take effect.
